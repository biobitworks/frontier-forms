Why data transformation helped—and where it did not

Positive mechanism

Typed, source-referenced FHIR facts and validated abstention reduce unsupported fills; L1×L3 gains are measured without changing neural weights.

OCR correction

Learning corrections to OCR output improved complete synthetic forms, while cropping with a trained YOLO detector alone lost accuracy.

Negative controls

OpenCV already recovered all known field boxes; trained logistic/RF challengers did not improve them. Drawing boxes before Apple OCR damaged recognition.
