Figure 1a

Workstream status by research stage

Rows: workstreams (merged PR numbers or open task IDs). Columns: the five stages a result must pass before it can be claimed. Hover a row for its key number.

Figure 1b

Mechanisms and combinations versus outcomes

Each panel: rows are mechanisms or combinations on identical cases, columns are outcome metrics (0 to 1). Cell shade encodes how good the value is: darker blue = better, so columns marked "lower better" are shaded by 1 − value. The printed number is always the raw value. Grey cells were not measured or not run; they are never zero.

Every result, admitted or not

One row per tested claim, including null, negative and failed results and successful attacks. Verdict is shown as icon + label. Counts by verdict are in the legend.
