Strict interpretation and promotion gates

A point plotted under “training” must be a real time-ordered checkpoint of the same model with matching source, split, oracle, decoding and scoring. Different models, training sizes or FCO modules must never be connected as a learning curve. The success criterion is a paired outcome gain with exact McNemar, patient-level clustered bootstrap CI excluding zero, multiple-comparison adjustment where relevant, an independent untouched cohort/template and safety guardrails. Discovery-only tests cannot pass this gate merely because p < 0.05.

A single form’s fields and multiple forms from one patient are correlated. Exact case-level McNemar is descriptive when patient clusters repeat. The patient-cluster bootstrap is the more appropriate uncertainty assessment; even that does not repair test-set leakage.
